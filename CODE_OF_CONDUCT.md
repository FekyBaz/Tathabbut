# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Commitment
We aim to make participation in Tathabbut a respectful, welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome evidence-based disagreement and corrections.
- Recognize that text classification is context-dependent.
- Protect other people's privacy.
- Give feedback about false positives without attacking contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, personal attacks, hateful or dehumanizing language directed at participants, doxxing, threats, and conduct that reasonably makes the community unsafe are not acceptable.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may remove or reject contributions that violate this code and restrict participation when necessary. They should aim to explain decisions clearly. Report incidents privately to project maintainers.
